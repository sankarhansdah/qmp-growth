## Product
- **Name:** Quotation Maker Pro (QMP). Site: https://quotationmaker.pro
- **What it is:** Android app to create quotes/estimates, invoices, proforma invoices, purchase
  orders, delivery notes and receipts, and send them as professional PDFs.
- **Category:** mobile quoting & invoicing for small businesses.
- **Status:** pre-launch on Google Play. iOS and web not available — never promise dates.
- **Pricing:** free plan, 5 documents/month. Paid plans: TODO (see data/product-facts.md).

## Ideal customers
1. **Tradespeople & contractors** (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, painting, carpentry, construction,
   solar, CCTV) who quote on site from their phone.
2. **Freelancers & service businesses** (designers, photographers, event, cleaning, interior).
3. **Small shops / traders** that send quotations and proforma invoices to B2B buyers.
Common trait: no accountant on hand, quoting from a phone, losing time on Word/Excel templates.

## Markets
US, UK, Canada, Australia first; India (GST) second; UAE next. See CLAUDE.md market table for
terminology, spelling and tax system per market.

## Problems we solve (in customer words — expand from research, never invent quotes)
- Making a quote in Word/Excel takes too long and looks unprofessional.
- Getting tax right (GST CGST/SGST/IGST, VAT, sales tax) and totals right by hand.
- Re-typing the same clients and items every time.
- Quoting on site with poor connectivity.

## Positioning
For contractors, freelancers and small businesses who quote from their phone, QMP turns a quote
into a clean, tax-correct PDF in under a minute — offline, in their country's format.
Unlike desktop invoicing suites, it is built for the phone and for quotes first.

## Differentiators (only claim what product-facts.md confirms)
- Per-country tax handling (GST split, VAT, sales tax) and document terms.
- Works offline, syncs later.
- Saved clients and items; professional templates with logo and signature.
