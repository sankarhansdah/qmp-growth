# Allowed sources for FACTS (rates, rules, thresholds, legal requirements)

Anything not on this list can be used for gap analysis or real-user language, never as the
source of a fact. Prefer the most specific official page; link deep, not to home pages.

## us
irs.gov, sba.gov, ftc.gov, state revenue departments (*.gov, e.g. tax.ny.gov, cdtfa.ca.gov,
comptroller.texas.gov), streamlinedsalestax.org, state licensing boards (*.gov)

## gb
gov.uk (HMRC guidance, VAT notices), legislation.gov.uk

## ca
canada.ca (CRA), provincial finance ministries (*.gov.on.ca, gov.bc.ca, etc.)

## au
ato.gov.au, business.gov.au, legislation.gov.au, abr.gov.au

## in
cbic-gst.gov.in, cbic.gov.in, gst.gov.in, gstcouncil.gov.in, incometax.gov.in, egazette.gov.in,
indiacode.nic.in

## ae
tax.gov.ae, u.ae, mof.gov.ae

## eu
taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu, eur-lex.europa.eu, national tax authority sites

## standards / general
iso.org (currency/country codes), developer.android.com / support.google.com (product/Play facts)
